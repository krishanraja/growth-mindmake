# Mindmake growth plan

Build a repeatable route to five qualified conversations each week and at least one paid proof in the first 30 days after activation. These are Krish's outcome targets, not a forecast or a promise to clients. Activation is the operator's recorded start, after access, source freshness and approval handling work. A repository creation date does not start the clock.

## What this engine owns

The engine finds relevant buying moments, helps relationships move with permission, prepares useful decision aids and individual drafts, and reports the pipeline. Control Center remains the private source for contacts, company intelligence, deals, actions and outcomes. This public repository contains methods, configuration, prompts, contracts and synthetic examples.

The `mindmake` repository keeps the website, free AI brief, lead delivery, commercial canon and capability. This engine consumes those services and facts. It does not recreate their delivery sequences or become a second CRM.

## The commercial route

A founder, principal or commercial leader owns an important decision. A verified change, real relationship or expressed problem makes Mindmake relevant. We help them understand one choice, then offer one appropriate next step. A qualified conversation may lead to one privately scoped paid proof that leaves a working system the leader can keep.

Two public capabilities provide the relevance: **Build your AI brain** and **Build your AI revenue engine**. Select the capability from the decision in front of the person. Preserve the current offer in the source repository. Never introduce an offer ladder, public fee, start date, availability claim or delivery promise.

The main public action is [Get your free AI brief](https://mindmake.co/?start=1). It means answer a few questions, keep the brief, then a free consultation. For a curious reader without a live problem, the separate route is [Make Your Mind Up](https://makeyourmindup.ai), using its Subscribe free button. Each message carries one ask. CTRL stays a private person-to-person invitation from Krish, with no public invitation, form, link or price.

## Acquisition portfolio

| Motion | Useful trigger | Work the engine prepares | Human decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Existing relationships | An actual last conversation and a relevant current decision | A private warm thought and a useful note | Whether the relationship warrants contact |
| Introductions | A connector can recognise one relevant person or situation | A permission request and a forwardable explanation | Two separate opt-ins before connecting people |
| Named cold prospects | A current primary source supports a specific commercial question | A bounded outside-in read, decision aid and individual draft | Whether this exact action is appropriate and lawful |
| Selected founder visibility | A cleared operating artifact or supported external argument | A post, talk angle or host pitch that helps a leader decide | Whether the public voice and context fit |
| Practice assets | A recurring decision can benefit more than one leader | A reusable decision aid or cleared proof-led explanation | Whether facts, proof rights and publication scope permit release |
| Inbound | An actual request, reply or fit digest | A human response and qualification brief | Whether to consult, scope a proof, refer or close |

The mixed model is deliberate: Krish carries relationships and selected public appearances; Mindmake carries reusable explanations, evidence and campaigns. The engine supplies a small review queue so the work can run without demanding daily founder content. The operator owns preparation and recording; Krish owns relationships and exact sends. Until a named operator accepts the runbook, Krish carries both roles and the capacity limit stays visible.

## First 30 days after activation

| Stage | Operator work | Evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before activation | Verify the private Control Center handoff, suppression handling, current canon and exact-action review. Run synthetic fixtures. Record the activation timestamp only when the operator starts the real queue. | No private material in Git; an inspectable draft has a source trail and a blocked send state |
| Days 1 to 7 | Read authorised existing relationships and inbound first. Add research only against an explicit question. Prepare warm opportunities and a small named cold set that can receive useful individual attention. | Actual conversations, accepted next steps and reasons a draft could not advance |
| Days 8 to 14 | Follow agreed next steps, obtain introduction opt-ins, refine the decision aid, and consult qualified leaders. | Qualification evidence, declined introductions, unanswered hypotheses and agreed proof decisions |
| Days 15 to 21 | Scope bounded proofs around a real decision. Use the strongest cleared learning for one trust asset if useful. | Written acceptance criteria, named decision owner, private commercial review and a next decision date |
| Days 22 to 30 | Close or release stalled opportunities, check payment evidence, and review channel contribution and founder effort. | At least one paid proof supported by payment evidence, or an explicit shortfall diagnosis |

These stages guide work; they do not schedule messages. A target shortfall never authorises more sends, weaker qualification or broader data collection.

## How we decide what to do next

First protect replies, requested information and promises already made. Next help a decision-ready relationship or introduced leader. Then prepare a named opportunity whose evidence and useful next step are clear. Prepare trust assets when they resolve a recurring buyer question. Research-only findings enrich the private intelligence store; they do not become a ranked outreach list merely because an enrichment job finished.

Prepare at most five new recommendations per daily run by default, with a hard maximum of ten. These are review-capacity limits, not sending quotas or assumed conversion rates. The operator can reduce the limit to match actual review capacity. Draft volume, impressions, contacts enriched and email opens measure work or exposure. They do not count as qualified conversations or revenue. Use [the scorecard](playbooks/07-scorecard-and-experiments.md) to diagnose the constraint before changing activity.
