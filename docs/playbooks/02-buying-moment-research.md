# Buying moments and research

Research should make a commercial situation clearer. It cannot establish a person's budget, private anxiety, willingness to buy or desire to hear from Mindmake.

## Start with a question

Use an authorised research brief with one company or bounded company question. Examples: a product now changes what customers pay for; a new pricing model may change the sales conversation; an expanding leadership remit may make trusted decision context useful. Cover ambition and expansion as well as pressure.

Prefer the company's own product, pricing, filings, releases and interviews. A hiring advert can support that a company advertised a role; it cannot prove the role exists today or that the business has a capability gap. Corroborate consequential claims independently where possible. An aggregator repeating the same release is not a second source.

## Keep an evidence ledger

For each observation record the URL or authorised private source reference, title, publisher, event date, retrieval timestamp with timezone, minimal relevant excerpt or faithful summary, public/private class, claim supported and limitation. Record missing event dates explicitly. Avoid copying entire pages, personal profiles or unrelated records.

Classify each resulting statement:

- **Observed fact:** the source directly establishes it within its scope.
- **Supported inference:** the evidence suggests it; state the inference and plausible alternative.
- **Unknown:** ask or research further if it changes the next decision.

A useful buying-moment brief states the decision owner role, changed condition, decision it may affect, present workaround if evidenced, consequence worth testing, alternative explanation, evidence age, missing evidence, and what one-page aid would help. Do not invent a cost estimate to make a weak trigger seem material.

## Two separate decisions

Research writes a proposed enrichment to private `company_intel`, with provenance and uncertainty. It leaves outreach eligibility undecided. A separate authorised relationship or prospect brief may use that evidence to assess whether to prepare a named action. Never generate or rank a sales queue simply because enrichment produced a fact.

Public company news does not confer permission to process unrelated personal data or make contact. See [privacy and suppression](09-privacy-and-suppression.md) before a named action progresses.

## Hold and refresh

Check a trigger is still current when it matters to a draft. An explicit deadline must come from a current source. If a material claim has only one source, say so. Quarantine contradictory facts. If the buying moment does not survive the strongest ordinary explanation, store the research and prepare no outreach.

The deliverable is a sourced hypothesis to test against the real business. It is never a finished diagnosis or advice. Close with the useful decision aid or missing research question, not a speculative claim that the prospect needs Mindmake.
