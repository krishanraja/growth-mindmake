# Scorecard and experiments

The outcome targets are five qualified conversations per week and at least one paid proof in the first 30 days after activation. Report the actual count, denominator, window and evidence coverage. Do not claim the engine produces these results because the code runs.

## Counting rules

Record `launch_date` explicitly as the actual activation date in `YYYY-MM-DD` form. Use Europe/London for operator dates and retain timezone-aware event timestamps. Weekly reporting uses Monday 00:00 to the following Monday 00:00, with an exclusive end, and reports week-to-date at the run time. Mark activation and current partial weeks as partial; do not extrapolate them to claim a full week of performance.

The first-month outcome covers 30 London calendar dates, including the activation date as day one. The runtime reports the first and last included dates. This date-based window follows the recorded activation date; it does not infer a launch timestamp. A missing activation date makes that outcome pending, not failed or passed. The build date never substitutes for activation.

Count at most one qualified conversation per person in a reporting week. A second meeting or decision with the same person that week does not inflate the count. The [qualification standard](06-inbound-qualification-and-proof.md) requires a held exchange, all five conditions and human confirmation by Krish; unknowns do not qualify. Preserve excluded records and reasons privately.

A paid proof needs a verified payment or paid deposit linked to the proof. Report unique paid proofs, pending proposals and reversals separately. Do not turn a meeting into a deal or a deal into cash. Public examples contain synthetic figures only.

## Weekly view

| Measure | What it tells us | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified conversations, target 5 | Whether meaningful decisions are moving | Dated exchanges with qualification evidence |
| Paid proofs in first 30 days, target at least 1 | Whether growth has produced an actual paid start | Payment evidence linked to the accepted proof |
| Requested or agreed next steps due | Promises we need to keep | Private action queue |
| Replies and held conversations | Where attention becomes a substantive exchange | Observed messages and conversation records |
| Qualified conversations by origin | Which motion contributes | First meaningful source plus assisted touches |
| Proposal decisions pending | Whether the next constraint is commercial | Private deal records and agreed dates |
| Drafts blocked, by reason | Missing evidence, access, fit or review capacity | Runtime validation and operator review |
| Founder review effort | Whether the engine saves attention | Actual time recorded, or explicitly unknown |
| Suppression, objections and failed delivery | Whether the route creates harm or noise | Authoritative private records |

Attribute warm relationship, introduction, named cold, inbound, post, speaking or publication using observed evidence. Preserve `unknown` when the source is unclear. Assisted touches may explain a result; they do not create extra conversations or deals.

## Diagnose a shortfall

If useful opportunities are scarce, inspect buying moments and access to decision owners. If drafts stall, inspect evidence quality and review effort. If people reply but do not qualify, inspect fit and argument. If qualified leaders do not progress, inspect the next decision and proof scope. If accepted scopes do not pay, inspect real commercial objections with Krish. More activity is a hypothesis, not the default cure.

Report missing source coverage before calculating ratios. A zero denominator yields `null`, not zero percent. Ratios describe the observed sample; they do not become a forecast. Opens and impressions can inform a channel discussion but never substitute for a qualified exchange.

## One experiment at a time

Write the experiment as the [copy and offer doctrine's test record](10-copy-and-offer-doctrine.md#test-record-mapped-to-the-weekly-experiment).

Record an experiment privately with a falsifiable hypothesis, eligible audience, one change, comparison method, leading signal, outcome signal, adverse signal, review date or event, owner and reversal condition. Use a decision aid angle or CTA change before considering a new paid tool or channel. Existing approval, privacy and sending boundaries still apply.

Low volume limits causal claims. Compare comparable opportunities and explain selection differences. Call directional learning directional. Do not optimise on a tiny apparent conversion uplift or alter qualification to improve a chart. At day 30, inspect actual paid evidence, missing data, relationship cost and founder effort before recommending the next experiment.
