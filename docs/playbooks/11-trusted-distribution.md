# Earn trust through people who multiply it

Accepted by Krish, 22 October 2026. This implements the current growth plan
and supersedes the handcrafted operating model of 19 October. The North Star
and original 18 October commercial activation window are preserved.

The route is useful evidence, trusted access, recognition, commercial
conversations, then paid work. A portfolio operator, institution or convener
is the primary acquisition unit. Individual prospecting tests problem fit.
Use media relationships as an advantage while testing other sectors.

Four connected motions share one capacity budget: multiplier relationships,
institutional distribution, external podcasts/stages/forums, and proof/demand.
Owned-show guest relationships are not guest appearances on another show.
Each campaign names its native Control Center identity, owner, dated evidence,
credible route, argument, cleared proof, exact first action, requested
commitment, preparation, follow-through and success measure. An invitation
must pass Visibility's existing audience, platform and distinctive-angle
standard. Fame alone cannot pass it. Never infer ownership, wealth, buying
authority, personal familiarity or permission from a title or network tier.

Allocate approximately 70 percent of fresh preparation to multipliers and
institutions and 30 percent to direct-buyer learning. Replies and commitments
go first. Review allocation fortnightly. The weekly strategic review has a
stable London-week identity and selects one bounded experiment concerning
missing institutions, comparable practitioners' routes to invitations,
unanswered buyer questions or introductions that stalled.

Mindmake extends leadership judgement and helps business decisions. A
workflow is the means of proving a capability, not the identity of the
business. A free pilot covers two weeks and one agreed workflow, with a
before-and-after check. State the leader's decision first. Define quality,
correction burden and preparation effort before starting. Wider portfolio
rollout is separately scoped. Use Krish's name for relationships and selected
appearances; use Mindmake for reusable proof and institutional recognition.

In-person opportunities are London and New York; virtual opportunities are
global. Ten held 30-minute intro meetings per week remains the target.
Valuable appearances and executive sessions may need additional founder time.
Five qualified conversations weekly and one paid proof in the original first
30 days remain separate targets. The working recognition target is two
qualified institutional/external appearances confirmed by 30 November, with
at least one held, reviewed after a baseline. Missing coverage is unknown.

Control Center owns private records, human edits, approvals and the single
action list. Its shared engine protocol supplies scoped context, source
coverage and complete outcome windows. Registry presence is not connector
usability. Do not create another CRM, pitch list, scheduler or provider.

Preparation is autonomous. A campaign plan is followed by a finished go-live
action bound to recipient identity and address, channel, wording, assets,
time window, native source revision and cost. An amended version is the
version to execute. Supported execution initially means a plain email,
without attachments or spend, through the existing Google connector with
send and read scopes. Other routes are visibly blocked. A durable claim
prevents retries after uncertainty. Only an independent provider read of the
sent recipient, subject, body and SENT label completes the action. A send
acknowledgement, draft, submission, booking, held meeting, qualified
conversation and verified payment are different facts.

Keep attribution on the existing engagement: first source and assisting
relationships may all contribute without creating multiple wins. Preserve
old copy and edits before any positioning revision; never turn new wording
into permission to send. A report or a successful scheduled run establishes
mechanics only. Repeated trusted access and commercial progress establish
whether the engine works.

## Amplifiers and the five rows (Rulings, Krish, 2026-10-26)

"Every action should make me exponentially more visible." The engine hunts
the people whose yes puts Krish in front of many, in his words: prolific
investors, well-connected people, people anywhere with inordinate amounts of
money and a desire to take control of AI and steer their investments towards
it ("think global"), those who deliver AI training en masse, those who educate
students en masse, those who educate CEOs en masse, and broadcast and social
channels with mass audiences on growing with AI. In the protocol these are
`investor`, `connector`, `ai_capital`, `ai_trainer`, `student_educator`,
`ceo_educator` and `broadcaster` (`src/stages/rows.mjs`).

- **Who.** Read from a person's own words on their record. A title is never
  proof of wealth, authority or permission: `ai_capital` needs a named capital
  vehicle (a family office, a sovereign or strategic fund) and an AI interest
  on record, and a chairman is never read as rich.
- **How far.** Reach is a sourced, dated figure (members, learners a year,
  listeners, portfolio companies), asked of Control Center's research desk as
  a cited question once a month for the strongest candidates. Without one it
  is a band from their followers, and without that it is unknown. Never a
  guess.
- **Rank.** `log10(reach) x fit x route x goal x closeness`: a million-strong
  channel ranks above a thousand-strong one by its logarithm, not by a
  factor of a thousand. Fit is Control Center's own Mindmake fit for the
  person (0 to 100, `mindmake_fit`), never a guess from their title; route is
  the channel a note can take; goal is a match to one of his week's goals,
  named word for word in `serves`.
- **Fit first** (Ruling, Krish, 2026-10-27: "I have no idea whether I want
  to do those things or not. Or why I should do those things"). Anyone who
  sells what Mindmake sells is left out, and so is anyone whose fit is under
  40 (`FIT_FLOOR`) or not yet scored. The first live rows were ranked by
  followers alone and put a 0-fit creator top of VISIBILITY and someone
  Control Center itself called "not a buyer" top of PARTNERS.
- **Every row says why** (protocol 1.7.0). `because` is the line under the
  title on his card, at most 80 characters (two lines): who they are and why
  them in one clause (Control Center's hook or why_them), then how he knows
  them (known since a year, last email a month, or no email on record); when
  both will not fit, why them stays whole and the known line shortens. It is
  built only from whole pieces (Krish's first live cards ended on "by.",
  "at." and "u.s."): the first sentence of the hook or why_them only if it
  fits whole, else "title at company", the title, or the title up to its
  first separator; a piece that would end on a joiner (at, of, by, and, the
  and the like), an initial or an internal slug is dropped, and the known
  line alone is better than a broken one. Raw lower-case titles are
  title-cased. The
  title is then at most 56 characters and leaves the company to that line. `brief` is what a tap opens,
  two to six lines: who, how he knows them, why them, the ask and the goal it
  serves, reach with its basis, and the fit. Written in code from what
  Control Center holds, never invented.
- **The full card** (protocol 1.8.0; Ruling, Krish, 2026-10-27: every
  suggestion opens a full card: who they are, how he knows them, why them
  now, what he gets, and the angles to take, each from the record with its
  source; a card that cannot say these is held, not shown). A tap opens:
  - *who*: Control Center's own sentence about them and their organisation,
    one or two whole sentences, else their title at their company;
  - *known*: since when and through what (his Gmail, LinkedIn connections,
    phone contacts, Instagram, Facebook; enrichment is never where he knows
    someone from), then the emails each way. "Each other" only when both
    wrote; "not written back" only on a counted zero; never "no email" when
    emails are counted. The brief's known line is the same sentence;
  - *why*: whole sentences, at most three: a close tie gone quiet and since
    when, the hook, why them, their company's why now, and a public post
    about AI in the last 120 days (never one selling AI);
  - *gain*: what a yes gets him by the ask, a number only with its source,
    ending on the goal it serves word for word;
  - *angles*: one to three, each titled in the canon's own words this run
    (the brain door, the revenue engine door, the homepage's intern to chief
    of staff line, Make Your Mind Up, the main ask) and tied to their record.
    A line the canon no longer carries is not offered;
  - *sources*: the pages the facts came from, at most six; never a profile,
    an address or a number.

  A card that cannot say who they are and why them is held as `thin_card`,
  and the engine asks Control Center once a month who they are (a `profile:`
  question inside the research caps); the answer fills the card next run.
  Going on someone's channel needs a channel they own named in their record
  (a podcast, newsletter, show or publication): followers alone are an
  audience, not a channel, and such a person is held as `no_channel`.
  An appearance names its show (`showOf`): from the record's facts or
  titles, or a cited answer to a `channel:` question asked once a month when
  the record says they own one but not what it is called (`unnamed_channel`).
  What he gets says the show by name, and its audience only where the figure
  counts its audience. His own shows, Signal & Noise and makeyourmindup, are
  never an appearance (`own_show`); their people may still be a tie to catch
  up with.
- **Facts, not verdicts** (the second live 1.8.0 run, 27 October 2026). A
  sentence that judges whether they fit ("not a fit for Mindmake", "not
  AI-related", "a direct fit", "budget signal", "relevant if the operator")
  is dropped, and a judge's clause after a fact ("..., though their website
  shows no hiring triggers") is cut back to the fact (`VERDICT`, `factOf`).
  The line under the title and the brief read the same facts. A first name
  is never a title ("Dr.", "Prof.", "Sir"). A partner's angles are tied to
  their company or what they do, and a partner with nothing known gets one.
- **A topic is what this one is about, or null** (`topicOf`, a small fixed
  list). A blanket topic would let one "Not my topic" refuse a whole row for
  good, so a close tie has no topic.
- **The ask decides the row.** Partner (investors, AI-minded capital, AI
  trainers) is PARTNERS; an introduction (connectors) and a signal from a
  buyer are REACH OUT; an appearance (broadcasters) is VISIBILITY; a session
  (CEO and student educators) is EVENTS; a close tie gone quiet for 90 days is
  NETWORK, read from Control Center's `quiet_ties` and its network, where the
  last contact is the later of a recorded touch and the last email (Control
  Center never wrote the touch, so NETWORK was empty until it read the
  email). Six a row at most, one person once.
- **Words.** One ask, in Krish's own name, British English, no invented
  familiarity, no em dash, and Mindmake only in its canon's own words. Ruling
  (Krish, 2026-10-27): pitches call him Executive Host of Signal & Noise. An
  appearance pitch says so, with his publication as the canon names it. The
  canon is read live from `krishanraja/mindmake` on every run
  (`src/stages/canon.mjs`), never from a pinned copy: the pinned copy went
  stale within a week of its approval. Each action names the canon files and
  the engine's own word files by git blob SHA in `source_revision`, and
  Control Center reads those SHAs again before he acts.
- **Channel.** An address on Control Center's record first (it never comes to
  the engine), WhatsApp only for a close tie, X only where his numeric id is
  on file. No route, no row.
- **Draft, don't send.** A row is a prepared send that he carries out himself
  from his phone: a Gmail draft he sends, a message app opened with the words
  typed, or a form submitted on his tap. The engine never sends it.
- **No, with a reason, for good.** Every No is kept with no decay. A plain No
  means never that person on that row; wrong person or already in touch means
  never that person; not now means 30 days; too small raises the reach floor
  for that kind to the next band; wrong audience drops that kind in that
  sector; not my topic drops that topic; pitch off moves that row to its other
  words, and a repeat is for the weekly review to put to him as a doctrine
  change.

## Signals from his buyers (Rulings, Krish, 2026-10-26)

Shown a LinkedIn post ("AI's next job casualty could be your boss"), Krish
said the people who engage with posts like it "could be really valuable to
reach out to but only if they're the right types of ICP: worried executives,
as opposed to AI people", that the voices he follows "are talking to the
people I want to talk to", and that "the growth engine needs to think about
those types of signals and relevant signals from the ICPs I want, and bring
them to me for action."

- **Where to look.** This week's posts by the voices he follows (Control
  Center's `voices`) and on a few topics where executives argue about AI and
  their jobs; then the comments under the posts where most people argued
  (`src/stages/signals.mjs`).
- **Who counts.** An executive (C-suite or senior by their own headline),
  worried in their own words (their board asking, their team, their managers,
  not knowing what it means), and never an AI person (someone who builds,
  sells or teaches AI), a consultant, coach or speaker selling to the same
  buyer, or someone whose comment pitches an answer. The Horizon's buyer, a
  PE or VC backed media, adtech or data leader, ranks first.
- **What he gets.** A REACH OUT row whose note opens with what they said and
  names the post, then Mindmake in the canon's words and the canon's main ask
  with its one link. One message, one ask, no sequence, sent only by him.
- **Who it reaches.** The canon limits outreach to his network, people
  introduced to him, readers and people who heard him speak, and says adding
  any other list "is Krish's decision". This is that decision, for people who
  engaged in public on the subject, one at a time, each on his own press
  (Ruling, Krish, 2026-10-26). Control Center files someone it finds this way
  as a cold, engaged contact, and keeps an address its search did not check
  off the record.
