# Trust, selected posts and speaking

Use public work to help leaders recognise a decision and judge how Mindmake thinks. The mixed model keeps selected personal observations and relationships in Krish's name, and reusable methods and explanations in Mindmake's voice.

## Choose a source before a format

Start with a cleared operating artifact, a permitted client pattern, or current primary evidence. Capture the decision, what changed, what failed or worked, and what the reader can now do. If no lived artifact exists, write a clearly labelled external commentary brief. Never invent an "I built", "we found" or client anecdote.

Select the smallest useful form:

| Job | Form | Reader payoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Share an earned operating lesson | A selected founder post | A concrete decision rule and its limits |
| Explain a recurring choice | Mindmake decision aid | A reusable test or comparison |
| Open a relevant room | A host pitch or talk abstract | A specific audience decision the session helps |
| Answer an engaged reader | An individual reply draft | A useful answer without a hidden campaign |

No daily publishing quota. A post can end on a useful verdict with no CTA. If it includes a commercial ask, use one route supported by the current canon. Mindmake says "we". Krish may say "I" in his own posts and notes when an authorised source earns the claim.

## LinkedIn

Verify the actual intended account, audience and surface before preparing a post for action. Do not imply the account has a cadence, audience size or growth record without current evidence. Avoid generic trend summaries, synthetic hooks, engagement bait and automated comments or connection requests. An operator may identify a relevant conversation, then prepare a useful reply for Krish's separate decision.

## Speaking and hosts

Read the host's first-party audience, programme, current date, submission rules and topic fit. Draft one angle that helps that audience make a particular choice. Explain the argument in miniature before biography. Use only cleared credentials and proof. Record the source for the event date and any deadline; a past event page never proves a current opportunity.

Prepare the exact host recipient, subject, pitch and one next step. No promise of audience reach, speaking fee, availability, recording rights or free consulting without Krish's decision. A host invitation does not consent the audience to sales follow-ups.

After an event, capture only authorised individual requests and opt-ins. Useful questions may become anonymous reusable topics after privacy review. Attendance proves attendance, never advisory client status.

## Publication boundary

Make Your Mind Up owns its editorial formats and publication operations. This engine may propose a sourced brief or distribution draft to that owner. It does not copy a sales pitch into an editorial channel or claim that a queued brief has been published. Verify the current destination and status before any subscription CTA.

## Release gate

Inspect facts, proof rights, voice, target account, links and the complete payload. Krish separately approves the exact post or host message immediately before manually publishing or sending. Store the observable result privately. A finished draft, accepted angle or scheduled review is not publication.
