## Doctrine
From DRIVE.md, Rules:
- One home per item: Strategies, reviews, measurements and exported assets live in Drive.
- One current version: A new version Control Center records as approved replaces the PDF in Current, and the old one moves to Growth archive in the same step with its superseded date added to its title.
- Drafts stay few: The last three drafts of an asset at most; older ones move to Growth archive.
- The index is the finish line: Every session that files something updates the Growth index before it ends, recording each Canva design's id when the design is made, so engine-made designs can always be told apart.
- Nothing loose: Only the READ ME and the Growth index sit at the top of 03_Growth.
- Never share, trash or move out: Sharing, changing permissions, trashing, or moving anything into or out of 03_Growth needs Krish's yes at the time.
- Canva mirrors it: Designs the engine makes live only in the Canva folder Mindmake Growth, in its subfolders Brand kit, Speaker and guest one-sheet, and Drafts.
- No people: A weekly review or readout filed here carries counts, paths and decisions, never names or contact details.
